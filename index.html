FAQ & NPCI Rules

Understanding MDR & UPI guidelines

Who pays the 0.4% fee above ₹2,000?

The merchant discount rate (MDR) is paid by merchants and commercial vendors to acquiring banks. UPI payments made by shoppers and individual consumers are free.

Bank Velocity Limits

NPCI limits savings bank accounts to 20 UPI transactions per 24 hours. Multiple rapid payments of identical amounts to the same VPA might be flagged by automated bank anti-fraud filters.

Progressive Web App Offline Mode

Splitzz installs directly on Android (Chrome) and iOS (Safari Share > Add to Home Screen) as a standalone app with offline support.
